## Gaming Font Tips

- Always test your font on the specific game before finalizing — some games may not support all Unicode characters
- Keep usernames readable — overly decorative fonts can be hard for teammates to type
- Use bold or sans styles for fast-paced games where readability matters
- Glitch and gothic styles work great for horror/survival game profiles

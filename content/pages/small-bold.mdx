## Small & Bold Font Styles

- **Math Sans** — clean, small sans-serif text
- **Subscript** — tiny text that sits below the line
- **Superscript** — small text that floats above the line
- **Small Caps** — miniature uppercase letters
- **Bold Serif** — heavy, classic bold text
- **Bold Sans** — strong, modern bold text
- **Bold Italic** — bold + slanted for emphasis
- **Sans Italic** — light, slanted text

## Where to Use Small & Bold Fonts

- **Footnotes and annotations** — subscript and superscript for references
- **Social media bios** — small text fits more content in limited space
- **Usernames** — tiny text for subtle, minimalist names
- **Captions** — bold text for emphasis on key words
- **Messages** — bold for emphasis, small for details
- **Designs** — varied text weights for visual hierarchy

## Tips for Small & Bold Text

- **Small fonts** are great for fitting more text in character-limited bios
- **Bold fonts** are perfect for headings and key words that need attention
- **Italic** adds personality and emphasis without being overwhelming
- Test small text on mobile — some devices may render tiny text differently
- Mix sizes: use bold for your name, small for your tagline

All **small fonts copy and paste** use Unicode mathematical alphanumeric symbols, which work on most modern devices. **Bold fonts** use Unicode bold characters that display consistently across platforms.

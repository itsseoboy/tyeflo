## Instagram Font Tips

- Keep bios readable — too many decorative characters can look cluttered
- Mix one fancy font with regular text for balance
- Test on your phone first — some older devices may render differently
- Use cursive for elegance, bubbles for cute, gothic for edgy
- Less is more — one styled word in a bio makes a bigger impact than all styled text
